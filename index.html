About

Executive leadership rooted in client relationships

Richard Dunn is an operations executive and business consultant with more than twenty years of continuous leadership experience inside iQor, progressing from front-line supervisor to Operations Director and Account Manager across multiple sites and verticals.

Throughout his career, the work has centered on one thing: client relationships. Understanding what clients actually need, communicating clearly when challenges arise, and consistently finding ways to grow the partnership once trust is established.

Currently leading operations in Medellín with direct responsibility for site integration, client delivery, and building leadership capacity across programs. The combination of long-tenure institutional knowledge and hands-on client management experience enables credible conversations at every level — with both clients and internal stakeholders.

Richard brings direct experience managing multimillion-dollar budgets, navigating contract negotiations and renewals, and leading bilingual, multi-national BPO operations with the distinct client expectations and compliance requirements of retail, telco, and financial services environments.

Consulting Philosophy

Trust first. Clarity always. Results that endure.

“The most durable client relationships are built on proactive communication, honest assessment of risk, and a clear point of view on the path forward — not on avoiding difficult conversations.”

Understand before you advise

Effective consulting starts with listening — understanding what clients actually need, not what assumptions suggest they want.

Surface issues early

Escalate risk to leadership with root cause, business impact, and a recommended path forward — before small problems become contract-level threats.

Grow what you protect

Proactive engagement and operational discipline don't just retain accounts — they create the conditions for scope expansion and long-term partnership.

Lead through people

Client experience is directly tied to team stability. Retention, development, and accountability at the leadership level are non-negotiable.
